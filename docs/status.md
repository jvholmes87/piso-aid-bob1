## Current priorities

Approve propulsion, power, and control architecture; reconcile sensor and physical interfaces; establish measurable acceptance criteria; complete bench integration; then progress through tethered, manual, and supervised waypoint tests.

Pre-funding trials are planned for South Korea at a site to be determined with required permission. Funded trials are planned for the United States.

Dates for fabrication and trials will follow scope, budget and resource confirmation. Prototype readiness will be reported using documented evidence rather than inferred from renderings.
