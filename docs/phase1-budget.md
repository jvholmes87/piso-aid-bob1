# Phase 1 budget: bare hull, weigh and float

Estimate prepared 7 October 2026 for pre-funding work in South Korea. Scope follows roadmap milestones 01–03 and the explorer build path: fabricate the pontoon assembly (BOM item 1 plus pad-eye plates, item 2), weigh it, and complete an unpowered calm-water float check. Propulsion, power, sensors, control and the autopilot stack are **out of scope** for Phase 1.

Frame welding and deck cutting are outsourced. The project lead assembles drums, pads, straps and deck.

Figures are retail prices for one unit, in KRW, VAT included unless noted. "Actual" and "Variance" columns are filled from receipts as purchases happen. No purchase has been made.

## Drum note (affects the hull estimate)

The retail 60 L drum priced above measures **325 mm inlet, 400 mm maximum width, 610 mm high** [4]. The September 2026 hull estimate and the explorer model an ideal **360 mm diameter × 589 mm** cylinder. The real drum is wider and tapered, which changes:

- buoyancy per unit draft (more width at the waterline than modelled);
- pad and strap geometry on drawings 0005–0006;
- drum-row spacing and therefore the initial-stability sensitivity cases.

Before fabrication, measure the chosen drum's outer profile and sealed volume and update the estimate inputs. This is the "exact drums" item in the estimate's close-before-fabrication list.

## Out of scope for Phase 1 (for planning only)

Propulsion, battery, autopilot (Cube Orange+), GPS, telemetry, RC, enclosures, cameras and lighting belong to bench integration and later phases. A separate estimate will be prepared when the control architecture is reconciled.

## What this estimate does not establish

Prices were read from public listings on 7 October 2026 and will change. No quotation has been requested. Fabricator pricing depends on drawing clarity, tolerance and travel; one written quote replaces line 1. Nothing here is a funding request or a claim that Phase 1 is funded.
