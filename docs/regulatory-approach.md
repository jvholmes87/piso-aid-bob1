# Regulatory approach

Bob 1 is a small test uncrewed surface vehicle (USV) intended to operate under the local waterway rules and required permissions applicable to each selected test site.

Pre-funding trials are planned for South Korea at a site to be determined, with required local permission. Funded trials are planned for the United States.

The full-scale concept is **crewed** and uses supervised autonomy. Under the current U.S. regulatory approach described in GAO testimony, the U.S. Coast Guard applies existing vessel regulations to autonomous vessels and does not have broad authority to waive statutory minimum crewing requirements outside a narrow pilot-program framework.

Long-term operational data from supervised operation is intended to support evidence-based discussions about future crewing, training, and operating standards. That is a potential research outcome, not an assumption that regulations will change.
