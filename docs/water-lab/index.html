UNDERSTAND THE MODEL

A view into the build.

This scene uses BOB 1’s drawing-based dimensions and ideal-cylinder flotation model. Vessel position uses calculated level draft. Ripples, reflections, and sound are procedural visual effects; they do not predict handling in waves.

Documented, assumed, measured.
Mass is a CAD estimate. Drum shape, spacing, and equipment heights remain provisional. No measured performance is shown.
