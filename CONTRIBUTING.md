# Collaboration
For an initial discussion, open a general inquiry describing your area of interest, relevant experience and proposed contribution. Do not include confidential information in a public issue.

Useful areas include marine/mechanical engineering, electrical integration, embedded software, robotics, fabrication and verification.

Discuss a proposed contribution before making major design changes. Documentation contributions should distinguish proposals from verified facts and link public claims to approved evidence. Detailed technical exchanges can move to a private channel by arrangement with Jason.

No contributor, contractor or partner relationship is established by opening an inquiry.
